## Micro USB To USB-C Upgrade
- ["The Original Steam Controller." - Micro USB To USB-C Upgrade (Timestamped)](https://youtu.be/DlmwQlGzo7E?si=3FSlf7PdMxRB4DtL&t=649)
    - Technical TL;DW:
        - [Soldering Guide](./Soldering.md)
        - De-solder antenna and micro USB port
        - Solder 4 wires for data +/-, power and ground to the old port's place and the new port
        - Get something to hold the new port in place like 2 component epoxy resin
        - Solder back the antenna
